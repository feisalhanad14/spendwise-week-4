SpendWise

Find out what's left of your budget.

Answer a few prompts about your monthly budget and spending. SpendWise adds up your expenses and shows your remaining balance in the browser console.

Open the console first: press F12, then choose the Console tab.

How it works

  1. Open your browser's developer console.
  2. Select Start budget check.
  3. Enter your name, budget and expenses when the prompts appear.
  4. Read your labeled results in the console.
